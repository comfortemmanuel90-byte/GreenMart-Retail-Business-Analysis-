# GreenMart-Retail-Business-Analysis-
GreenMart Retail Ltd — Business Performance Investigation

## Project Overview

This project investigates the business performance of GreenMart Retail Ltd, a retail company experiencing declining profitability despite relatively stable sales volumes.

The analysis focuses on identifying business challenges and developing data-driven recommendations to improve profitability, operational efficiency, and customer satisfaction.

## Business Problems

- Declining profit margins.
- Uneven sales performance across branches and product categories.
- Increasing customer complaints.
- Delivery performance challenges.
- Inventory management concerns.
- Inconsistent marketing results.
- Employee turnover in some locations.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI

- DAX

## Datasets Analyzed

- Sales Data

- Employee Data
- Inventory Data
- Marketing Data
- Delivery Performance

## Key Performance Areas

- Revenue and profitability analysis.
- Branch and product category performance.
- Customer complaints and satisfaction.
- Delivery performance.
- Inventory monitoring.
- Marketing effectiveness.
- Employee performance.

## Project Deliverables

- Interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Data Cleaning Report.
- Business Analysis Report.
- Business recommendations.

## Key Recommendations

The project focuses on identifying opportunities to improve product category performance, optimize inventory, reduce delivery delays, address customer complaints, improve employee performance, and strengthen marketing effectiveness.
